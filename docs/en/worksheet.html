Money and Banking, AI session 2 | 2026-11-18 (Wed) 11:00–11:50 | Wen-Sheng Chen (Center for General Education, National Dong Hwa University)

0 What we do today

  1. Hand JPMorgan Chase’s annual report to an AI and ask it for a table of nine numbers, each with the page it came from.
  2. Compute the five ratios yourself, then pick three cells and check them in the original: units, year column, consolidated or not.
  3. Run one stress test: work it out first on paper with T-accounts (a two-column sheet: assets on the left, liabilities and equity on the right), then ask the AI and mark which of its sentences come from the statement and which are its own inference.

What to bring

  • A laptop or a phone (a laptop makes the tables easier to read).
  • An AI that can take a PDF upload, logged in before class: Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM; notebook.google.com, with a Google account), or ChatGPT, or Claude. The same prompt works in all three.
  • A calculator, or the spreadsheet “checkup-template.xlsx” (on the course e-Learning site; type the numbers and the ratios compute themselves).

Prompt ③ (Bank check-up)
Answer using only the JPMorgan Chase 2025 Annual Report I uploaded. Use nothing else.
Make a table of these items from the Consolidated balance sheets at December 31, 2025 (units as printed; say that they are US$ millions):
Total assets; Loans, net of allowance for loan losses; Available-for-sale securities; Held-to-maturity securities; Investment securities, net of allowance for credit losses; Trading assets; Deposits; Total liabilities; Total stockholders' equity.
After each number write: the PDF page it is on, the page number printed on that page, and the line name exactly as printed.
Then write out the formula for each of these five ratios (which cell over which cell); I will compute the ratios myself:
1. Loans ÷ Total assets
2. Securities ÷ Total assets (use "Investment securities, net"; tell me in one sentence whether trading assets should be included and why)
3. Loans ÷ Deposits
4. Deposits ÷ Total liabilities
5. Equity ÷ Total assets
Finish with one sentence: which column (year) you used and whether this is the consolidated statement.
If you cannot find a number, write "not found". Do not fill it in from any other source.
Prompt ④ (Stress test)
Continue from the table above. Suppose 10% of deposits are withdrawn tomorrow, and the bank pays first out of "Cash and due from banks" and "Deposits with banks" (take both lines from the same report I uploaded, and give the PDF page for each).
1. Which balance-sheet lines move, and by how much? Show it as T-accounts.
2. Is there enough cash? If not, where does the rest come from?
3. What do the five ratios become?
Label every sentence: a number from the statement, your own calculation, or your inference?

3 If you get stuck

Open for common problems
  • Gemini Notebook will not open (school account blocked, cannot sign in): switch to ChatGPT or Claude, upload the same PDF, paste the same prompt.
  • The upload fails: 364 pages may be too large for some chat tools. Give it only PDF page 199, or use Gemini Notebook.
  • A number cannot be found: look at the page the AI named, and check which numbering it used — PDF page 199 is printed as page 167.
  • The AI says “not found”: it may be right. Open the page and look yourself.
  • Your ratios differ a lot from a classmate’s: the usual four causes are PDF page vs printed page, gross loans (“Loans”) instead of “Loans, net of allowance for loan losses”, trading assets counted as securities or not, and copying the 2024 column.
  • Anything else: paste the whole screen or the full error message into the AI and ask what to do. Never paste your account or password.
  • Nothing works: follow the teacher’s demo and compute the numbers with a calculator.
