What a bank check-up is: one sheet, five ratios

Other
Liabilities and equity
Deposits③ ④
Borrowing and other liabilities④

#RatioThe question
①Loans ÷ Total assetsDoes the money mainly go into loans?
②Securities ÷ Total assetsHow much is parked in securities?
③Loans ÷ DepositsLoan-to-deposit ratio: how much of the deposits is lent out?
④Deposits ÷ Total liabilitiesFunded by deposits, or by borrowing?
⑤Equity ÷ Total assetsHow thick is the equity cushion? (not risk-weighted; not the capital adequacy ratio)
